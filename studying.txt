unit circle

sin of A = opp / hyp => opp / 1 => opp (X divided by 1 is always X)
sin of A = opp (in unit circle)

--
in unit circle with:
- a graph of Y vertical axis
- t horizontal axis
- t being the position in the circle outline/circumference
- y being the side opposite the angle from origin
=> y is the sin of the angle from origin
=> y/sin goes back and forth periodically based on t, because t causes the angle to change as it moves
=> at Pi/2 (90 deg), the opposite side is the same as the hyp (1/1), so the sin/opp is 1
=> at 0, the opposite side is 0, so sin in 0
=> at pi/4 (45), the opposite side is half the hyp, sin 0.5

360 deg = 2 pi / OR 4pi/2 (four quarters) divided by 2
0 deg = 0 pi
45 deg = pi/4
90 = pi/2
180 = pi/2 + pi/2 (2pi/2) => pi
270 = pi/2 + pi/2 + pi/2 => 3pi/2
